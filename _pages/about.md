I am an petrologist/isotope geochemist by the day, trying to figure out the stories of the rocks that cooled from the molten Earth. When the lab coat is off, I make music, click pictures and cook some Telugu food for my soul. 

My current research interests are crust-mantle evolution and subduction zone volcanism.
